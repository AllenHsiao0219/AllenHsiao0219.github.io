設計師簡介 / ABOUT

用最克制的視覺語言，傳遞最具張力的品牌核心價值。
Using the most restrained visual language to convey the most compelling core brand values.

我是 Allen Hsiao。專注於現代簡約主義視覺規劃。擅長將繁複的訊息解構，轉化為兼具美感與實用性的平面視覺、品牌識別及網頁UI介面。我相信好的設計不是試圖加上什麼，而是無法再減去什麼。
I'm Allen Hsiao, specializing in modern minimalist visual design. I excel at deconstructing complex information and transforming it into aesthetically pleasing and functional graphic designs, brand identities, and web UIs. I believe good design isn't about adding anything, but about eliminating everything else.
